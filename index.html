Desi Low-GI Golden Rules

Simple Indian principles to prevent insulin resistance

  • The Salad First Rule: Sabse pehle cucumber, tomato, mooli khao. Iska soluble fiber gut me gel banata hai, jisse roti/chawal ka glucose slow absorb hota hai.
  • Switch Wheat to Millets: Normal gehun ke aate me Ragi, Jowar ya Besan milao (Multigrain ratio: 60% millets, 40% wheat) for lower GI index.
  • Add Protein to Every Thali: Katori bhar Dal, Dahi, Paneer ya Soyakhand har meal me zaroor rakhein. Protein slows down gastric emptying.
  • 10-Minute Post-Meal Shatapavali: Khane ke baad turant soyein ya baithein mat. 100 kadam (10 minute) tahalna blood sugar ko muscles me bhej deta hai.
